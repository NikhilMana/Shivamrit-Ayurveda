Version: 1.0

# Project Vision

Create India's most premium Ayurvedic wellness website that blends ancient Ayurvedic philosophy with modern luxury digital experiences.

The website should not feel like a traditional ecommerce store.

Instead, it should feel like entering a sacred Ayurvedic forest where users slowly discover the brand through immersive storytelling, cinematic animations, premium product photography, and elegant interactions.

The experience should evoke trust, serenity, luxury, authenticity, and timeless craftsmanship.

---

# Mission

Build a world-class digital flagship store that establishes Shivamrit Ayurveda as a premium Ayurvedic lifestyle brand through immersive storytelling, elegant product presentation, seamless ecommerce, and personalized customer experiences.
