# Content Philosophy

Every page should educate before it sells.

The website should feel like an immersive journey rather than a traditional ecommerce catalog.

Content should be:

- Calm
- Elegant
- Educational
- Inspirational
- Minimal
- Trustworthy
- Premium

Avoid marketing clichés.

Instead of aggressively selling, inspire users through storytelling, ingredient education, and craftsmanship.

# CONTENT WRITING GUIDELINES

- Write in a calm, premium, and trustworthy tone.
- Use short, elegant paragraphs.
- Educate before selling.
- Avoid exaggerated claims.
- Focus on authenticity, craftsmanship, and natural ingredients.
- Maintain a consistent luxury editorial voice across all pages.
